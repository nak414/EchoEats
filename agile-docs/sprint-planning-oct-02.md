## Meeting Purpose

We held this meeting after Dr. Sara approved our EchoEats project to agree on what we would work on during Sprint 1, select the main features, and divide the tasks among the group.

## Discussion and Decisions

1. Sprint Goal: Naeema started by asking everyone what we wanted to achieve during Sprint 1. We discussed what we could realistically complete and agreed to focus on planning and designing EchoEats rather than starting development.

Our goal was to complete the user stories, functional and non-functional requirements, initial architecture diagram, and wireframes. This would prepare us for developing EchoEats in the next two sprints.

2. Selected Features

We discussed the main features consumers and vendors would need. Jude focused on registration, finding nearby discounted food, searching and filtering, viewing food details and prices, reservations, and payments.

Salman focused on vendor accounts, adding and managing listings, updating quantities, setting prices, dynamic pricing, and managing reservations and pickups.

We also agreed that dynamic pricing was an important feature. Prices would decrease as the pickup deadline gets closer while considering customer demand and the vendor's minimum price.

After discussing the features, we selected six consumer and six vendor user stories for Sprint 1.

3. Task Distribution:Naeema led the discussion on dividing the work. We considered everyone's skills and preferences, discussed the proposed tasks together, and agreed on each member's responsibilities.

4. Team Organization: We agreed to use Microsoft Teams for communication, Jira to organize and track tasks, and GitHub to store project files and keep track of contributions.

5. Deadline: We agreed to prepare our first assigned tasks by October 6 and meet again to review our progress and plan the remaining work.
