# EchoEats
Software design and engineering - EchoEats application

EchoEats is a Dynamic Pricing Food Waste Marketplace that links local restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and home cooks with the cost conscious consumer to reduce food waste across the globe. 30-40% of the food supply is wasted every year. EchoEats offers real time pricing to give consumers the "true market value" in which vendors can sell their excess.
The traditional surprise bag has fared on recent smart, dynamic pricing delivery services which employ real-time algorithms that decay their prices the longer or the more unfavourable the current circumstances become. EchoEats allows vendors to cut costs and increase sales, and consumers to access quality meals at lower prices with instant updates, a sense of where vendors are located and the opportunities to gamify sustainability.
