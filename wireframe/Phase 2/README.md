For phase 2 I took my phase 1 wireframes and made them high fidelity, and I added the screens that were missing so the whole order flow is there. I kept the same colours, font and header so everything still looks like the same app.
There are five screens and one extra one:
Explore Food. The home page where you see cheap food near you. You can search, pick a category or filter, and each card shows the old and new price, how many portions are left and a View Details button.
Food Details. Shows the photo, description, dietary tags, vendor, pickup address and pickup time, the old and new price, the discount and how many are left. Reserve takes you to the reservation page.
Reservation. The food is held for you for 15 minutes at the same price. You can pick how many you want, see the total and a timer, and there's a warning that it gets cancelled if you don't pay in time.
Checkout. Shows your order, the price, the pickup info and how much time is left. It says it's a demo checkout so no real payment happens. Confirm Checkout takes you to the pickup page.
Pickup Confirmation. Shows that your order is confirmed, your pickup code, what you paid, the vendor address, the pickup time and how to collect your food.
Reservation Expired (extra). This shows up if you don't pay within 15 minutes. It tells you the reservation was cancelled and lets you reserve again.
User flow: Explore Food > Food Details > Reservation > Checkout > Pickup Confirmation. If you don't pay in 15 minutes you go to Reservation Expired instead. The diagram is in User flow.png.
