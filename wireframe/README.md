EchoEats UI Wireframes
By Mariam Ghaiballa
Figma prototype: https://www.figma.com/design/jsRDqp5ZL8hOCiuFltYoIT/QLB---design?node-id=10-229&t=yqK65RgjzyLi8etP-1 
This folder has the wireframes for EchoEats, our app where food vendors sell leftover food and the price goes down the closer it gets to expiring. They show how the main screens look for customers and vendors, and how you move between them. They’re rough layouts, not the finished app. I also connected them in Figma so you can click through them (link above).
There are six screens:
Login/Registration. I made two versions, one for customers and one for vendors, so each one goes to the right screen in the Figma prototype.
Consumer Home. Search, filters and nearby deals with the old price crossed out.
Food Details. Photo, vendor, description, dietary tags, prices, pickup time and the Reserve button.
Reservation/Checkout. The reserved food, a 15 minute timer, the price summary and payment options.
Vendor Dashboard. The vendor’s listings, quantities, current prices, reservations and the button to add a new listing.
Add Food Listing. The form to add food with a photo, quantity, starting price, minimum price, expiry and pickup time.
For the design I used the Kadwa font. The main colours are 
#63B04C for the header, 
#2E602F for buttons and headings, 
#EEFBEB for the cards, 
#FBFFF4 for the background, 
#B4C5AE for the bottom bar and 
#7C1515 for the timer. They match our logo. All screens use the same header and buttons so everything looks consistent.
